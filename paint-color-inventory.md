# Craft Paint Color Inventory

Standard matte acrylic colors only: DecoArt Americana Acrylics, FolkArt Acrylic Colors, and Delta Ceramcoat Acrylic Paint. Metallic, glitter, glossy/satin, outdoor, chalk, glass, and other specialty lines are excluded.

The **Own it?** boxes are intentionally unchecked so you can mark your own bottles. Hex values are approximate screen references derived from paint names; they are not manufacturer color measurements and will not match wet or dried paint exactly. Use the physical bottle/sample for final color decisions.

**Catalog check:** 2026-10-17. Color names and item codes were taken from the manufacturers' standard-color product listings. Americana codes are DecoArt DA/DAO codes; FolkArt and Ceramcoat codes are the base paint item numbers, not package-size SKUs.
