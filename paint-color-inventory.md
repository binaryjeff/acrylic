# Craft Paint Color Inventory

Standard matte acrylic colors only: DecoArt Americana Acrylics, FolkArt Acrylic Colors, and Delta Ceramcoat Acrylic Paint. Metallic, glitter, glossy/satin, outdoor, chalk, glass, and other specialty lines are excluded.

Each card shows a larger color swatch with the paint name, paint code, and CSS hex overlaid in high-contrast text. Cards are grouped by hue family within each brand based on their displayed CSS colors; FolkArt hex values remain approximate, so its grouping is approximate. Mark colors you own by adding `<sup>✓</sup>` immediately after the card image; leave it off for colors you do not own. The Americana section is limited to colors named in the two [supplied chart images](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71U1279CknL._AC_SL1500_.jpg) and [second chart image](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71QoWz0t4vL._AC_SL1500_.jpg); their hex values are sampled from the chart swatches. The Ceramcoat section includes all unique standard colors shown in its [first supplied chart](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81ocvwXg-YL._AC_SL1500_.jpg) and [second supplied chart](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81xSAxkvGdL._AC_SL1500_.jpg); repeated name/code swatches are listed once, and metallic and pearl finishes are excluded. Ceramcoat hex values are sampled from the chart swatches. JPEG and display variation means these are not manufacturer color measurements and will not match wet or dried paint exactly. Use the physical bottle/sample for final color decisions.

**Catalog check:** 2026-10-17. Color names and item codes were taken from the manufacturers' standard-color product listings. Americana codes are DecoArt DA/DAO codes; FolkArt and Ceramcoat codes are the base paint item numbers, not package-size SKUs.
